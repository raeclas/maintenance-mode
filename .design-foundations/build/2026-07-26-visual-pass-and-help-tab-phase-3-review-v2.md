## Distinctiveness (ai-tells.md CHECKER mode)
Aesthetic direction is nameable in three words: **MapleStory-carpentry-on-Bloomberg-density**. A thick tinted window frame with four corner rivets, a raised title-bar groove in the room's own hue, a gold letter-glyph plate per window, and a ten-hue Warden walk are all choices a generic system would not make — they are cited, derived (OKLCH-solved luminance-matched lanes), and specific to this project's own register (REMAKE-DESIGN.md §16). Not competent-but-generic. No distinctiveness Critical.
